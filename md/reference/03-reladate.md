### Concept
- Based on the days since he was born
- Used for personal reference, primarily “internal” use
- Uses base 10 
- The idea is not that everyone should use this relative to Maxwell, but relative to themselves

### Date Format
`a.b.c.d`

Where each of the letters are as follows:
a. 0-9 days 
b. 0-9 "rela-weeks" (comprised of 10 days)
c. 0-9 "rela-months" (comprised of 10 weeks)
d. 0-x "rela-years" (comprised of 10 months) 

#### Example 
20 years in days is 7305

5 days
0 rela-weeks 
3 rela-months 
7 rela-years 
 
So the date is written as 5.0.3.7 R.D.

100 years is 36500
0 days
0 weeks
5 months 
36 years 

So it's 0.0.5.36 R.D.
